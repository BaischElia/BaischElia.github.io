Music driven Software Engineering

Music production is one of my strongest creative outlets. I produce my own tracks using FL Studio by Image Line. High quality plugins are essential, but often expensive.

Coming from a software development background, I decided to design and build my own VST3 plugins using JUCE, C++ and AI assisted development.

Vibe Coding with Purpose

Audio plugin development is complex. By combining my own coding experience with AI assisted workflows, I was able to prototype faster, explore DSP concepts deeply and build clean, maintainable architectures.
